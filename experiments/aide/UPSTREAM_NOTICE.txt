RSISys loads the independently installed, pinned source checkout at runtime.
No AIDE implementation is vendored into this package. Its Agent.step,
search_policy, plan/code generation, review parsing, Journal and final selection
execute directly from that checkout. The execution callback is replaced with a
registered RSISys Lab operation. The optional local-provider transport supplies
enable_thinking=false and converts system-only prompts to user turns with the
exact same prompt text for Qwen chat-template compatibility. The GSM8K task and
inference broker are RSISys adapters.
