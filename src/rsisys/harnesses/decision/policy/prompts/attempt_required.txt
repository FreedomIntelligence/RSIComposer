Every exploration directive and these research operations MUST belong to an Attempt: ${operations}. Missing membership is rejected before execution. An Attempt is a testable research idea and its execution, including prompt, data, training, inference or harness improvements; it need not succeed or train weights. Public queries, waiting, project-wide notes and stopping may omit membership. Group related research decisions into an attempt for one idea. A top-level research_attempt_title (at most 500 characters) with no research_attempt_id starts a NEW research attempt; the harness assigns its UUID. Describe the hypothesis or approach in the title, not an operation name. Reuse its known research_attempt_id (at most 200 characters) from decision_graph across decisions and review invocations within this run, through modification, evaluation, comparison and adoption. For example, optimizing a prompt, benchmarking it and deciding adoption belong to one attempt. Alternative competing variants can use separate attempts. The title may be omitted on continuation; if supplied it must exactly match the canonical title. Never invent an attempt ID or rename an existing attempt. A title alone always starts a new attempt, even if another has that title. membership is never inferred from dependencies, candidates or history order.
